How It Works

Using PriceEngine is incredibly simple:

  • Step 1: Search — Enter any product name, brand, or model in the search bar. You can also search by image using our visual search feature.
  • Step 2: Compare — View prices from multiple retailers side by side. Filter by price range, store, shipping location, and more.
  • Step 3: Save — Click through to the retailer with the best price and complete your purchase directly on their website.

Our platform also tracks price history, shows customer ratings, and highlights special deals and discounts so you never miss a saving opportunity.

Our Story

PriceEngine was born from a simple frustration: spending hours jumping between different shopping websites just to find the best price. Our founder noticed that the same product could vary in price by 20%, 30%, or even 50% between different retailers, yet there was no easy way to see all those prices in one place.

We built PriceEngine to solve that problem. Today, our platform processes millions of price checks every month, helping shoppers across five countries save money on everything from everyday essentials to big-ticket electronics.
