Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2024

At PriceEngine, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our website.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to provide our price comparison service:

  • Search queries — The products you search for on our platform
  • Usage data — Pages visited, time spent on site, clicks
  • Device information — Browser type, device type, IP address (anonymized)
  • Account data — Email address (only if you create an account)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide price comparison results
  • Improve our service and user experience
  • Personalize search results and recommendations
  • Analyze site usage trends
  • Prevent fraud and abuse

3. Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your experience. Cookies help us remember your preferences, recently viewed products, and search history. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect site functionality.

4. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services including:

  • Google Analytics — for site usage analysis
  • Google AdSense — for displaying advertisements
  • Retailer affiliate programs — we earn commissions when you click through to retailers

These services may collect data according to their own privacy policies.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data. All data transmission is encrypted using SSL/TLS. We never sell your personal information to third parties.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of cookies
  • Contact us with privacy concerns

7. Children's Privacy

PriceEngine is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Contact page.
