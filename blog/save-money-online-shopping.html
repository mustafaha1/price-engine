10 Proven Ways to Save Money When Shopping Online

Published: October 24, 2024 | Reading time: 8 minutes

Online shopping is convenient, but are you really getting the best deal? With the right strategies, you can slash your online shopping bills by 20-30% without sacrificing quality. In this comprehensive guide, we share 10 proven money-saving techniques that savvy shoppers use every day.

1. Always Compare Prices Before You Buy

This is the golden rule of online shopping. The same product can vary dramatically in price between different retailers. A pair of Nike trainers might be £85 on Amazon, £72 on eBay, and £69 at a specialist sports retailer.

Before making any purchase over £20, spend 60 seconds comparing prices across at least three major retailers. Tools like PriceEngine make this instant by showing you prices from Amazon, eBay, Walmart, AliExpress, and dozens more in one search.

Pro Tip: Don't just compare the listed price. Factor in shipping costs, delivery times, and return policies. A slightly higher price with free next-day delivery might be better value than the cheapest option with £5 shipping and a 2-week wait.

2. Time Your Purchases Strategically

Retailers follow predictable sales cycles throughout the year. Knowing when to buy can save you hundreds of pounds:

  • January: Boxing Day sales continue, plus new year clearance events
  • March-April: Spring sales and Easter promotions
  • July: Amazon Prime Day and mid-year sales
  • November: Black Friday and Cyber Monday — the biggest discounts of the year
  • December: Pre-Christmas sales and post-Christmas Boxing Day deals

For electronics specifically, new models typically launch in September-October, meaning last year's models drop in price by 20-40%.

3. Use Cashback Websites

Cashback sites like TopCashback and Quidco give you money back on purchases you'd make anyway. Typical cashback rates range from 2% to 15% depending on the retailer.

Here's how it works: instead of going directly to a retailer's website, you visit the cashback site first, click their tracked link to the retailer, and complete your purchase normally. The cashback site records your visit and pays you a percentage of your spend back, usually within 8-12 weeks.

Over a year, regular online shoppers can earn £100-300 in cashback on purchases they were already planning to make.

4. Hunt for Coupon Codes

Before checking out, always search for a discount code. A quick Google search for "[retailer name] discount code" often reveals active codes for 10-20% off.

Popular coupon sites include:

  • HotUKDeals (UK-specific deals and codes)
  • VoucherCodes
  • RetailMeNot
  • Groupon

Browser extensions like Honey and Pouch automatically test coupon codes at checkout, saving you the manual search.

5. Abandon Your Shopping Cart

This sneaky tactic actually works. Add items to your shopping cart, enter your email address, then close the tab without completing the purchase.

Within 24-48 hours, many retailers will email you a discount code to entice you back. Typical offers include 10-15% off or free shipping. This works particularly well with fashion retailers like ASOS, Boohoo, and Shein.

6. Sign Up for Newsletters (Strategically)

Most online retailers offer a 10-20% discount for new newsletter subscribers. Create a dedicated email address for shopping, sign up for the newsletter, grab your discount code, then unsubscribe after your purchase.

Some retailers also offer birthday discounts, so set your birthday to a month when you're planning a big purchase.

7. Use Price Tracking Tools

Prices fluctuate constantly. A product that's £150 today might be £120 next week. Price tracking tools monitor products and alert you when prices drop.

Many price comparison sites, including PriceEngine, show price history graphs so you can see if the current price is genuinely a good deal or if you should wait for a better offer.

8. Check for Student, NHS, and Military Discounts

If you're a student, NHS worker, or military personnel, you could be missing out on significant savings. Many major retailers offer exclusive discounts:

  • Students: UNiDAYS and Student Beans offer discounts at hundreds of retailers (typically 10-20%)
  • NHS staff: NHS discounts available at many fashion, electronics, and home retailers
  • Military: Defence Discount Service offers savings across multiple categories

Always check if a retailer offers a specific discount before paying full price.

9. Consider Refurbished and Open-Box Items

For electronics, refurbished items can offer massive savings. A refurbished iPhone from Apple's official store comes with a new battery, new outer shell, full one-year warranty, and costs 15-30% less than new.

Amazon Warehouse sells open-box and returned items at significant discounts, and eBay's certified refurbished program offers like-new products with warranties.

10. Stack Multiple Savings Methods

The real magic happens when you combine multiple techniques. Here's an example of stacked savings on a £200 purchase:

  • Find the retailer with the lowest base price using PriceEngine: save £25
  • Use a cashback site offering 8% back: save £14
  • Apply a newsletter discount code for 15% off: save £26
  • Pay with a cashback credit card offering 1% back: save £1.35

Total saved: £66.35 (33% off!)

Remember: The best deal isn't always the cheapest price. Consider total cost including shipping, return policies, warranty coverage, and delivery speed. A slightly higher price from a retailer with excellent customer service might save you money and hassle in the long run.
