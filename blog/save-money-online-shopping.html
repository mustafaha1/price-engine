10 Proven Ways to Save Money When Shopping Online

Published: October 2024 | Reading time: 5 minutes | PriceEngine Blog

Online shopping has revolutionised the way we buy products, offering convenience, variety, and often better prices than high street stores. However, with so many retailers competing for your attention, it's easy to overspend or miss out on genuine deals. This guide reveals ten proven strategies that can help you save money on every online purchase.

1. Always Compare Prices Before You Buy

Price comparison is the single most effective way to save money online. A product might cost £50 on one website and £35 on another for the exact same item. Using a dedicated price comparison tool like PriceEngine lets you see prices from Amazon, eBay, Currys, Argos, and dozens of other retailers in one place.

Pro Tip: Don't just check the price. Factor in delivery costs, return policies, and warranty terms when comparing.

2. Use Browser Extensions for Automatic Coupons

Browser extensions like Honey, Capital One Shopping, and Rakuten automatically find and apply discount codes at checkout. These tools scan the web for valid coupon codes and test them all in seconds, potentially saving you 5-20% with zero effort.

3. Sign Up for Retailer Newsletters

Most online retailers offer a welcome discount (typically 10-15%) when you subscribe to their email newsletter. Create a dedicated email address for shopping to keep your inbox clean while collecting these introductory offers.

4. Abandon Your Shopping Cart

Leave items in your cart without completing the purchase. Many retailers send follow-up emails with discount codes (often 10-20% off) to encourage you to complete the order. This works particularly well with fashion and electronics retailers.

5. Shop During Sales Events

Mark your calendar for major sales events: Black Friday (November), Cyber Monday, Boxing Day sales, and mid-season sales. Prices during these periods can be 30-70% lower than usual. Use PriceEngine to track prices in the weeks leading up to sales.

6. Use Cashback Websites

Websites like TopCashback and Quidco give you money back on purchases. Typical cashback rates range from 1-10% depending on the retailer. Over a year, this can add up to hundreds of pounds in savings.

7. Check for Student and NHS Discounts

If you're a student or NHS worker, you have access to exclusive discounts through platforms like UNiDAYS, Student Beans, and Health Service Discounts. Many major retailers offer 10-20% off with these programmes.

8. Buy Refurbished or Open-Box Items

For electronics, consider buying certified refurbished products. These items are tested, cleaned, and often come with warranties at 20-40% below retail price. Apple's certified refurbished store and Amazon Renewed are excellent starting points.

9. Use Price History Tools

Tools like CamelCamelCamel (for Amazon) track price history, showing you whether today's price is genuinely a good deal or just marketing. PriceEngine also displays historical pricing data to help you make informed decisions.

10. Negotiate on High-Value Items

For expensive purchases over £500, don't be afraid to contact customer service and ask for a better price. Many retailers have discretionary discounts available, especially if you mention a competitor's lower price.
