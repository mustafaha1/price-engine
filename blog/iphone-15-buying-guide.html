iPhone 15 Buying Guide: Compare New, Used and Refurbished Offers

Originally published: 2024-10-22 · Updated: 10 November 2026 · By PriceEngine

The iPhone 15 family remains worth comparing if you want an older iPhone at a potentially lower price. In 2026, listings can include new old stock, used phones and refurbished devices. The cheapest listing is not necessarily the best-value one.

New, used or refurbished?

New

Check whether the item is sealed, the seller's warranty, the region of the device and whether it is genuinely unused. Older-model stock can be limited, so do not assume every retailer still sells it new.

Used

Ask about battery health, screen damage, repairs, network locks and whether Activation Lock has been removed. Confirm that the phone can be activated by the buyer before finalising a private sale.

Refurbished

Read the seller's grading definition. Refurbished does not always mean a new battery, original accessories or manufacturer certification. Check what testing was performed and what warranty is included.

How to compare iPhone 15 offers

  1. Choose the exact model and storage size.
  2. Decide whether you will accept used or refurbished condition.
  3. Search iPhone 15 offers on PriceEngine and inspect each relevant result.
  4. Open the seller's listing and confirm the model, colour, condition, shipping destination and price.
  5. Calculate the delivered total and review returns, warranty and seller feedback.
Watch for mismatched listings: A low-priced case, replacement screen, locked handset or damaged device is not a comparable iPhone offer. Verify the product title and description carefully.

Should you buy an iPhone 15 in 2026?

It depends on the price difference versus newer models, the condition of the handset and how long you expect to keep it. Compare camera features, battery health, software support and repair costs, not just the purchase price. If the savings are small, a newer model may offer better long-term value.

Questions to ask before checkout

  • Is this the exact iPhone 15 variant and storage capacity I want?
  • Is it new, used, refurbished or sold for parts?
  • What is the battery health and repair history, if applicable?
  • Is it unlocked and free from Activation Lock?
  • What is the total delivered price and the return process?

This is a buying guide, not a live price survey. Retailer offers and policies change. Confirm current details on the seller's product page.
