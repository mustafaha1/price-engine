iPhone 15 Buying Guide: Where to Find the Best Deals

Published: October 2024 | Reading time: 6 minutes | PriceEngine Blog

The iPhone 15 represents one of Apple's most significant upgrades in years, featuring the new USB-C port, improved camera system, and the powerful A16 Bionic chip. However, with prices starting at £799, finding the best deal can save you hundreds of pounds. This comprehensive guide shows you exactly where to look.

Where to Buy: Retailer Comparison

Here's where PriceEngine helps you compare prices across major UK retailers:

1. Amazon UK

Amazon frequently offers competitive pricing, especially during Prime Day and Black Friday. Look for "Amazon's Choice" listings and check seller ratings carefully. Amazon also offers trade-in programmes where you can get up to £600 credit for your old iPhone.

2. eBay

eBay is excellent for finding deals on new, sealed iPhones from authorised resellers. Many sellers offer "Buy It Now" prices below Apple's retail price. Always check the seller's feedback score and look for listings with "eBay Money Back Guarantee."

3. Currys

Currys often bundles iPhones with accessories or offers extended warranties. They also price-match major competitors, so if you find a lower price elsewhere, Currys will match it. Their "Care & Repair" service provides peace of mind for accidental damage.

4. Argos

Argos offers convenient same-day collection from thousands of locations. They frequently run promotions with voucher codes for £50-£100 off when you spend over a certain amount. Check their "Deals" section regularly.

5. Mobile Networks (Contract Deals)

EE, Vodafone, O2, and Three all offer iPhone 15 on contract. While the monthly payments seem manageable, calculate the total cost over 24 months. Sometimes buying outright and using a SIM-only deal works out cheaper by £200-£400.

Money-Saving Tip: Use PriceEngine to compare the total cost of contract vs. SIM-free purchases. The cheapest option isn't always obvious!

Best Times to Buy

  • Black Friday (November): Discounts of £100-£200 are common
  • Boxing Day Sales: January sales often match Black Friday prices
  • Back to School (August-September): Student discounts available
  • After New iPhone Launch: Previous generation prices drop by 15-20%

Trade-In Programmes

Apple's official trade-in programme offers competitive values, but don't stop there. Compare offers from:

  • Apple Trade In
  • Amazon Trade-In
  • MusicMagpie
  • Envirofone

Differences can be £50-£100 depending on your current phone's condition.

Refurbished Options

Apple's Certified Refurbished store offers iPhone 14 models at 15-20% below retail with full warranty. For the iPhone 15 specifically, check Amazon Renewed and reputable refurbishers like Back Market for near-new condition devices at significant savings.
