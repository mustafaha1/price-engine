iPhone 15 Buying Guide: Where to Find the Best Deals

Published: October 22, 2024 | Reading time: 10 minutes

The iPhone 15 is one of Apple's most popular smartphones, but with prices starting at £799 for the base model, finding the best deal can save you hundreds of pounds. In this comprehensive buying guide, we compare prices across all major UK retailers and share insider tips on how to get the lowest price.

Where to Buy: Retailer Price Comparison

Prices for the iPhone 15 vary significantly between retailers. Here's what to expect across the major UK sellers:

1. Apple Store (Official)

The Apple Store sells the iPhone 15 at full retail price with the option of 0% APR financing over 24 months. While you won't find discounts here, you do get:

  • Apple's 14-day return policy (even if opened)
  • AppleCare+ enrollment at purchase
  • Trade-in credit for your old iPhone (up to £650)
  • Free engraving

Price: £799 (no discount, but best trade-in values)

2. Amazon UK

Amazon frequently offers modest discounts on iPhones, especially during Prime Day and Black Friday. Look for "Amazon's Choice" listings from authorised sellers.

  • Prime members get free next-day delivery
  • 30-day return policy
  • Monthly payment options available
  • Bundle deals with accessories sometimes available

Typical Price: £749-£779 (small discounts, occasional bigger sales)

3. Currys

Currys is one of the UK's largest electronics retailers and often runs iPhone promotions, especially when bundled with accessories or contracts.

  • Regular promotional codes (10-15% off)
  • Price match promise against major competitors
  • NEXT DAY delivery available
  • In-store support and setup assistance

Typical Price: £729-£769 (frequent promotions)

4. eBay (Authorised Sellers)

eBay can offer significant savings if you buy from authorised resellers or during promotional events. Look for sellers with high feedback ratings.

  • eBay Money Back Guarantee protects your purchase
  • Regular 10-20% off site-wide coupon events
  • Refurbished options with warranties available
  • auction listings for potential bargains

Typical Price: £699-£749 (with coupons, potentially lower)

5. John Lewis

John Lewis matches Apple's pricing but adds their famous 2-year warranty at no extra cost — a significant value add.

  • 2-year warranty included (vs Apple's 1 year)
  • 35-day return policy
  • Expert advice in-store
  • My John Lewis members get occasional exclusive offers

Price: £799 (same as Apple, but 2-year warranty adds value)

6. Mobile Networks (Contract Deals)

If you prefer spreading the cost, contract deals from networks like EE, Three, Vodafone, and O2 bundle the phone with a monthly data plan.

  • No large upfront payment required
  • Includes data, calls, and texts
  • Upgrade options after 24 months
  • Sometimes include free streaming subscriptions

Total Cost: Usually £200-£400 more than buying outright over 24 months

Money-Saving Tips for iPhone 15

Trade In Your Old Phone

Apple offers competitive trade-in values, but don't automatically accept their offer. Compare trade-in prices:

  • Apple Trade In: Up to £650 for recent iPhones
  • MusicMagpie: Often beats Apple for older models
  • CEX: Instant cash, though sometimes lower values
  • Selling privately on eBay/Facebook: Highest return but more effort

Consider Refurbished

Apple's certified refurbished iPhones come with a new battery, new outer shell, full one-year warranty, and all original accessories. Savings range from 15-30%.

Warning: Only buy refurbished iPhones from reputable sources. Avoid deals that seem too good to be true — they often involve stolen devices or fake replicas.

Choose the Right Storage

The 128GB base model is sufficient for most users. Apple charges £100-£200 for storage upgrades that cost them a fraction of that. Consider:

  • 128GB: Fine for average users who use cloud storage
  • 256GB: Good for photography enthusiasts
  • 512GB+: Only necessary if you store lots of 4K video locally

Saving £100 by choosing 128GB over 256GB is often the smartest financial decision.

Final Verdict: Where to Buy

For the absolute lowest price: eBay during a coupon event or Currys with a promotional code.

For peace of mind: John Lewis (2-year warranty) or Apple directly (best support).

For convenience: Amazon Prime for next-day delivery and easy returns.

For spreading the cost: Mobile network contracts, though you'll pay more overall.
