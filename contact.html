Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use PriceEngine?

Simply enter a product name in the search bar on our homepage and press enter. We'll show you prices from multiple retailers side by side. Click "Buy Now" on the cheapest option to complete your purchase.

Is PriceEngine free to use?

Yes! PriceEngine is completely free for shoppers. We earn commissions from retailers when you click through and make a purchase, which helps us keep the service free.

Why are the prices different from what I see on the retailer's website?

Prices change frequently throughout the day. While we update our data regularly, there may be a slight delay. Always verify the final price on the retailer's website before completing your purchase.

Can I list my products on PriceEngine?

We don't host product listings directly. However, if you're a retailer interested in partnership opportunities, please email us at partners@priceengineapp.com.

How do I report an incorrect price?

We appreciate your help in keeping our data accurate. Please use the contact form above and select "Report a Bug" as the subject. Include the product name, expected price, and retailer.

Response Time: We aim to respond to all enquiries within 24-48 hours during business days. For urgent matters, please include "URGENT" in your subject line.
